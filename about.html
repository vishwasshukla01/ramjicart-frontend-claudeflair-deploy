About Ramjicart

Ramjicart is an online platform that helps customers discover participating restaurants and grocery shops and place food or grocery orders.

How it works

Customers can browse participating businesses, choose available items, and place pickup or delivery orders where those options are offered. Restaurants and shops manage the products they list and fulfil accepted orders. Delivery orders may be handled by an assigned delivery partner.

Our aim

We aim to make it easier to find local options and follow an order from placement through fulfilment. Available businesses, items, prices, and delivery coverage can vary by location and are shown on the website.

Get in touch

For questions or support, visit our Contact & Support page. Please include your order number when your question relates to an order.
